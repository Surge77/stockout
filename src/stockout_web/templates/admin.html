The two models are graded on different scales, because they answer different kinds of question. Predicting a number is scored by how much of the day-to-day variation the model explains — 1.00 would be perfect, 0.00 no better than always answering with the average. Predicting a label is scored by how often it picks the right one. Neither scale applies to the other, so each card names the one its own task produces rather than printing a figure that cannot exist.
